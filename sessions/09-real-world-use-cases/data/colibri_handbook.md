# Colibrí Online University — Student Program Handbook 2026

*Colibrí Online University is a fictional organization created for teaching. All names, rules and figures in this handbook are invented. Do not use it as real academic or financial advice.*

## 1. About the university

Colibrí Online University offers fully online undergraduate certificates and short professional programs in business, data and education. All courses run on the Colibrí learning platform. Each academic year has three terms of twelve weeks: the Winter term starts in January, the Spring term in May and the Fall term in September. Classes combine recorded lessons, weekly live seminars and graded assignments.

## 2. Programs and study load

A standard course is worth 3 credits and requires about 9 hours of work per week. Full-time students take 4 courses per term; part-time students take 1 or 2 courses per term. Students who want to take more than 4 courses in one term need written approval from their program director. Every program has an academic advisor who helps students plan their courses.

## 3. Enrollment and deadlines

Regular enrollment closes one week before the term starts. Late enrollment is possible until 10 calendar days after the first day of the term, with a late fee of 40 US dollars. After that date no new enrollments are accepted for the term. Students can add or drop a course without academic penalty during the first 14 calendar days of the term.

## 4. Tuition and payment plans

Tuition is charged per credit. Students can pay the full term at enrollment or choose the payment plan, which splits the term into three monthly installments with no interest. The first installment is due at enrollment. If an installment is more than 30 days late, the student cannot register for the next term until the balance is paid. Scholarships are handled by the Financial Aid Office, not by teaching staff.

## 5. Withdrawals and refunds

Students who withdraw before the term starts receive a 100% tuition refund. Students who withdraw during weeks 1 and 2 receive a 50% refund. From week 3 onwards there is no refund. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 20 business days. The late enrollment fee is never refunded.

## 6. Assessment, grading and extensions

Courses are graded on a 0–100 scale and the passing grade is 60. Each course publishes its assessment plan in the first week. Students can request an extension for an assignment through the student portal at least 48 hours before the deadline; extensions are granted for up to 7 days. Late work without an approved extension loses 10 points per day. Grade appeals must be submitted within 5 business days after grades are published.

## 7. Academic integrity and the use of AI tools

Students may use AI assistants for brainstorming, feedback on drafts and practice, unless the course syllabus says otherwise. Every assignment must include a short AI use statement that names the tool and explains how it was used. Submitting AI-generated work as your own without declaring it is an integrity violation. Suspected violations are reviewed by the Academic Integrity Committee, which may ask the student to explain their work in a short oral check.

## 8. Tutoring and student support

Free online tutoring is available for writing, statistics and programming from Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00 (Bogotá time), and on Saturdays from 9:00 to 13:00. Students book sessions of 30 minutes in the student portal. Each student can book up to 4 tutoring sessions per week. Wellbeing counseling is also free and confidential.

## 9. Accessibility accommodations

Students with a disability or a health condition can request accommodations such as extra exam time, captioned videos or alternative formats. Requests are made through the Accessibility Office form in the student portal and are answered within 10 business days. Students should request accommodations at least 3 weeks before the first exam of the term. Medical details are shared only with the Accessibility Office, never with instructors.

## 10. Technical support

The Help Desk answers platform and login problems 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, through the chat in the student portal. If the platform fails during a timed exam, students must report it to the Help Desk within 2 hours so the exam can be reopened.

## 11. Privacy and student records

Colibrí Online University processes student data only for academic and administrative purposes. Staff and virtual assistants never ask students for passwords. Grades and personal records are shared only with the student, unless the student gives written authorization.

## 12. When to contact a person

The virtual assistant answers general questions about this handbook. Questions about individual grades, payments, disciplinary cases or personal situations must be handled by a person: students should contact Student Services through the portal, which answers within 2 business days.
