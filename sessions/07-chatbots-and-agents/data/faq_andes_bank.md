# Andes Bank — Customer Service FAQ

> **Fictional company.** Andes Bank is an invented digital bank created for teaching purposes in the course *Desarrollo y Evaluación de Modelos Propios* (Universidad del Rosario). Every product, fee, limit, channel and address in this document is made up. Do not use it as real financial information.
>
> Format: each `###` heading is a customer question and the paragraph below it is the approved answer. Amounts are in Colombian pesos (COP) unless stated otherwise.

## About Andes Bank and service channels

### What is Andes Bank?
Andes Bank is a digital bank that operates in Colombia through its mobile app and website. It offers savings accounts, debit and credit cards, transfers, bill payments and small personal loans. Andes Bank has no physical branches.

### How can I contact customer service?
You can chat with a human advisor 24/7 in the app (Help → Talk to an advisor) or call the Andes Line from the app (Help → Call us). You can also write to support@andesbank.example; email requests are answered within 2 business days.

### What are the customer service hours?
Chat and phone advisors are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The complaints and disputes team works Monday to Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

### Where can I deposit or withdraw cash if there are no branches?
You can deposit and withdraw cash at Punto Andes correspondents (partner convenience stores) and withdraw cash at ATMs of the Andes partner network. The app shows the nearest locations (Cash → Find a location).

## Accounts

### How do I open a savings account?
Download the Andes Bank app, register with your Colombian ID (cédula) and take a selfie to verify your identity. Most accounts are approved in less than 10 minutes. You must be 18 or older.

### Is there a minimum balance or a monthly fee?
No. The Andes Savings account has no minimum balance and no monthly fee.

### What interest does the savings account pay?
The Andes Savings account pays 3.5% effective annual interest on your balance. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly. The rate can change; the app always shows the current rate.

### Can I have more than one account?
Each customer has one savings account, and you can create up to 3 savings pockets inside it to separate money for different goals. Pockets pay the same interest as the main account.

### How do I update my phone number, email or address?
Go to Profile → Personal details in the app. Changes to your phone number or email require a one-time code sent to the new contact. Changing your legal name requires identity verification by video call with an advisor.

### How do I close my account?
Go to Profile → Close account. Your balance must be zero, or you can transfer it to another account first. Closing is free and takes up to 3 business days. Cards linked to the account are cancelled automatically.

## Cards

### How do I block my card if it is lost or stolen?
Block it immediately in the app: Cards → select the card → Block card. Blocking is instant and free. If you cannot access the app, call the Andes Line or chat with an advisor 24/7 and they will block it for you. After blocking, you can request a replacement.

### How do I request a replacement card?
After blocking your card, go to Cards → Request replacement. The first replacement each year is free; additional replacements cost COP 18,000. The new card is sent to your registered address.

### When will my new card arrive?
New and replacement cards arrive in 5 to 7 business days in main cities and in up to 10 business days in other municipalities. You can track the delivery in the app (Cards → Track delivery).

### How do I activate my card?
Go to Cards → Activate card, enter the last 4 digits printed on the card and create your 4-digit PIN. The card works as soon as it is activated.

### How do I change my PIN?
Go to Cards → Change PIN in the app, or change it at any ATM of the Andes partner network. Never share your PIN with anyone: Andes Bank will never ask for it.

### My PIN is blocked after three wrong attempts. What do I do?
Your PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts. Unblock it in the app (Cards → Unblock PIN) after verifying your identity with a selfie. You do not need a new card.

### What is the difference between the debit card and the credit card?
The Andes Debit card spends the money in your savings account and has no fees. The Andes Credit card gives you a credit line from COP 500,000 to COP 15,000,000, depending on a credit assessment. The credit card has no handling fee in any month in which you make at least 5 purchases; otherwise the handling fee is COP 12,000 for that month. The interest rate is shown in the app and on your statement and never exceeds the legal maximum rate.

### Can I use my card abroad?
Yes. Turn on International purchases in the app (Cards → Settings) before you travel. Purchases in foreign currency have a 2% currency conversion fee.

### Do you offer virtual cards?
Yes. Every customer gets a free virtual debit card in the app, available immediately after opening the account. Use it for online purchases and freeze it at any time.

### There is a card payment I do not recognize. What should I do?
Block your card in the app first. Then open the transaction (Movements → select the payment → Report a problem) and choose "I don't recognize this payment". Andes Bank investigates and gives you an answer within 15 business days.

### I was charged twice for the same purchase. What should I do?
Often one of the two charges is a pending authorization that disappears within 3 business days. If both charges are still there after 3 business days, report it from the transaction (Report a problem). If the duplicate is confirmed, the money is returned within 10 business days.

### An ATM kept my card. What should I do?
Block the card in the app and request a replacement. For security, cards retained by ATMs are destroyed by the ATM operator and cannot be returned.

## Transfers and payments

### How do I send money to another Andes Bank customer?
Go to Transfers → To an Andes customer and use their phone number. Transfers between Andes Bank accounts are instant, free and available 24/7.

### How long do transfers to other banks take?
Transfers to other Colombian banks made before 3:00 p.m. on a business day arrive the same day. Transfers made after 3:00 p.m., on weekends or on holidays arrive the next business day.

### How much do transfers to other banks cost?
The first 10 transfers to other banks each month are free. After that, each transfer costs COP 3,500. Transfers to other Andes Bank customers are always free.

### What are my transfer limits?
The default daily limit for transfers is COP 10,000,000. You can lower it at any time in the app (Profile → Limits). You can raise it up to COP 20,000,000 after an additional identity verification.

### Can I cancel a transfer?
Transfers to Andes Bank customers are instant and cannot be cancelled. Transfers to other banks can be cancelled in the app while their status is "Scheduled".

### My transfer has not arrived. What should I do?
Check its status in Movements. If the status is "Completed" and the recipient still does not see the money after 1 business day, open the transfer and select Report a problem to request a trace. You will receive an answer within 5 business days.

### How do I pay my bills?
Go to Payments → Pay bills, then scan the barcode of the bill or search for the company. Bill payments are free and you can schedule recurring payments.

### Can I receive money from abroad?
Yes. You can receive international transfers in US dollars; they are converted to pesos at the exchange rate of the day the money arrives. Each incoming international transfer has a fee of USD 15 and usually arrives in 1 to 3 business days.

## Cash deposits and withdrawals

### How do I deposit cash?
Deposit cash at any Punto Andes correspondent using your phone number or the deposit code from the app. The first 5 deposits each month are free; after that, each deposit costs COP 2,000. The maximum per deposit is COP 3,000,000.

### How much does it cost to withdraw cash?
You get 3 free withdrawals per month at ATMs of the Andes partner network; after that, each withdrawal costs COP 4,900. Withdrawals at other ATMs cost COP 7,500 each.

### What is the daily withdrawal limit?
You can withdraw up to COP 2,000,000 per day at ATMs and Punto Andes correspondents combined.

### Can I withdraw cash without a card?
Yes. Generate a cardless withdrawal code in the app (Cash → Withdraw without card). The code is valid for 30 minutes at ATMs of the Andes partner network.

## Loans and credit

### Does Andes Bank offer personal loans?
Yes. Andes Flex loans go from COP 1,000,000 to COP 30,000,000, with terms from 6 to 48 months. The interest rate depends on your credit profile. You apply in the app and receive a decision within 24 hours.

### Can I pay off my loan early?
Yes. You can make extra payments or pay off your Andes Flex loan at any time without penalty.

### What happens if I pay my loan or credit card late?
Late-payment interest applies from the first day of delay and never exceeds the legal maximum rate. Andes Bank always notifies you before reporting a delay to credit bureaus. If you expect to have trouble paying, talk to an advisor to explore a payment agreement.

### Does Andes Bank offer mortgages, investment funds or cryptocurrencies?
No. Andes Bank does not offer mortgages, investment funds or cryptocurrency products, and it does not give investment advice.

## Security and fraud

### Will Andes Bank ever ask for my password or PIN?
Never. Andes Bank will never ask for your password, PIN, full card number or one-time codes by phone, chat, email or text message. If someone asks for them, it is fraud: do not share anything and report it.

### How do I report a suspicious message or call?
Do not click links or share codes. Report it in the app (Help → Report fraud) or forward the message to fraud@andesbank.example. The fraud team works 24/7.

### I think someone accessed my account. What should I do?
Block your cards in the app, change your password and close all active sessions (Profile → Security → Sign out of all devices). Then chat with an advisor 24/7 so the fraud team can review your recent movements.

### How do I reset my app password?
Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen and verify your identity with a selfie. Your new password must have at least 8 characters.

### How does two-step verification work?
When you sign in on a new device or make a transfer above COP 1,000,000, the app asks for a one-time code sent by text message to your registered phone. The code expires after 5 minutes. Never share it with anyone.

## Complaints

### How do I file a complaint?
File it in the app (Help → File a complaint) or write to complaints@andesbank.example. You receive a case number immediately and an answer within 15 business days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can take your case to Andes Bank's Financial Consumer Ombudsman, whose contact details are in the app.
