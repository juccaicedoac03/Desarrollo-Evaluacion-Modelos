Three knobs of decoding

Temperature

Sharpens or flattens the distribution.

Top-k

Keeps only the k most likely tokens.

Seed

Makes random sampling reproducible.

Math · KaTeX

Softmax with temperature

Given logits \(z_1, \dots, z_V\) and a temperature \(T > 0\):

$$ p_i = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_{j=1}^{V} \exp(z_j / T)} $$

\(T \to 0\)

All probability goes to the arg max (greedy decoding).

\(T \to \infty\)

The distribution becomes uniform over the vocabulary.

Prices stay as text when wrapped: US$ 20 per month vs US$ 35 per month.

Code · highlight.js

The same idea in NumPy


import numpy as np

def softmax(logits, temperature=1.0):
    """Turn raw scores into probabilities (numerically stable)."""
    z = np.asarray(logits, dtype=float) / temperature
    z -= z.max()                      # subtract the max: no overflow
    p = np.exp(z)
    return p / p.sum()

logits = [3.2, 2.6, 2.4, 1.6]
for t in (0.5, 1.0, 1.5):
    print(t, softmax(logits, t).round(3))

Wrap-up

Key takeaways

  • Softmax turns logits into probabilities; temperature rescales them first.
  • Top-k removes the long tail before sampling.
  • A fixed seed makes sampling reproducible and comparable.
